Keydoku is free, offline Sudoku for Android keypad phones. This update improves game history, timekeeping, and Android compatibility.

### What's improved

- Undo and redo keep your elapsed time and settings intact.
- Completed puzzles count once in statistics, even after undo/redo or reopening the app.
- Hints cannot change a paused puzzle.
- The timer keeps time between key presses and excludes time away from the game.
- The board accounts for system bars and display cutouts.
- Touch selection reports click actions to Android accessibility services.
- Local progress is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

### Install and update

Requires **Android 6.0 or newer** and a **physical keypad or keyboard**. Touch selects cells and menus; there is no on-screen number pad.

Download **keydoku.apk** below. **SHA256SUMS** provides its SHA-256 checksum. For updates through Obtainium, add `https://github.com/d4mysus/Keydoku`.

This version establishes the signing key for future updates. If you installed the older 0.1.0 preview, uninstall it once before installing this APK. Uninstalling clears local progress.

No ads, accounts, analytics, or network permission. All features remain free; donations are optional. Source is available under GPL-3.0-only.

Found a problem? [Report an issue](https://github.com/d4mysus/Keydoku/issues) with your phone model, Android version, and reproduction steps.
