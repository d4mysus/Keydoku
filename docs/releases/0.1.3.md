Sudoku, now with a darker side.

### New: Appearance settings

Open **Settings → Appearance** and choose:

- **System** (default): follow your phone's light or dark appearance on Android 10+. Older Android versions default to light.
- **Light**: keep the original light appearance.
- **Dark**: use dark backgrounds with readable digits, notes, grid lines, and cell highlights.

Your choice is saved and applies immediately without resetting your puzzle, timer, notes, or undo/redo history. Menus, native dialogs, and system bars follow the selected appearance. Menus now scroll to keep every setting reachable on smaller screens.

### Install or update

Download **keydoku.apk** below. Requires **Android 6.0 or newer** and a **physical keypad or keyboard**; there is no on-screen number pad.

Install over 0.1.1 or 0.1.2 to keep your progress and settings. The signing key is unchanged. Users of the older 0.1.0 preview must uninstall it first, which clears local progress.

For updates through Obtainium, add `https://github.com/d4mysus/Keydoku`. **SHA256SUMS** contains the APK's SHA-256 checksum.
