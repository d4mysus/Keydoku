Keydoku is a free, offline Sudoku game designed for small Android keypad phones.

Use the D-pad to move around the board, number keys to enter digits, * to toggle notes, and # for a hint. Touch can select cells and menu items. A physical keypad or keyboard is required for digit entry.

Features include Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert puzzles, notes, hints, undo and redo, a timer, and local statistics.

No ads, accounts, analytics, or network permission. Game progress and settings stay on your device. Optional Bitcoin and Buy Me a Coffee donations support development and do not unlock any features.

Source code is available under GPL-3.0-only.
