# Keydoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Keydoku is an offline Sudoku game. This policy describes the Android app.

## Data collection

Keydoku does not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal data. It has no
accounts, ads, analytics, or network permission, and does not access location,
contacts, camera, or microphone data.

## Local game data

The app stores puzzle progress, notes, settings, elapsed time, and game
statistics on your device to run the game and restore your session.
Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled. There is no online
synchronization.

Clear Keydoku's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to delete
this data. Deleted progress cannot be recovered by the developer.

## Optional donations

Bitcoin donation information is displayed as an address and a locally generated
QR code. Keydoku does not initiate payments or transmit wallet information.

Buy Me a Coffee opens an external website in your browser when you select
the link. Information you provide there is handled by that service under its
own privacy policy. Donations are optional and do not unlock features.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through
[Keydoku's GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/d4mysus/Keydoku/issues).
Do not post personal information in public issues.
