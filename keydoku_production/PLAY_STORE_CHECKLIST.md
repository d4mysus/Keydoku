# Deferred Google Play checklist

Google Play distribution is not part of the current release plan.
Use [distribution and signing](../docs/DISTRIBUTION.md) for GitHub APK releases
and Obtainium updates.

If a Google Play launch is considered later, review the policies current at
that time and prepare:

- A verified developer account, app record, and Play App Signing configuration.
- A properly signed Android App Bundle and appropriate version code.
- A hosted privacy policy and the required developer contact details.
- Accurate Data safety, content-rating, target-audience, and advertising declarations.
- Listing text, icon, feature graphic, and gameplay screenshots.
- Any required account-specific testing and production-access prerequisites.
- A fresh payments-policy review of the donation options.

Do not treat this checklist as confirmation of Play eligibility or approval.
The current GitHub signing key must remain available regardless of any future
store signing arrangements.
